# 01 — Line Plan

## Strategic position

Do not launch "women's gymwear." That category is owned by Gymshark, Alphalete, and
Lululemon, all of whom outspend King Killers by orders of magnitude and all of whom are
selling the same aesthetic.

Launch **womenswear for women who train to fight.** BJJ, MMA, boxing, Muay Thai —
plus the strength and conditioning work around it. That market is real, underserved,
and it is the market King Killers already has permission to speak to. A fighter-owned
Tampa brand with a splatter aesthetic has no credibility problem here and no chance in
generic athleisure.

Practical consequences of that position:

- **Rashguards and fight shorts are in the hero tier**, not the afterthought tier. They
  are the products no mainstream brand cuts properly for women.
- **Sizing goes to 2XL.** Most combat-sports brands stop at L for women. Extending the
  range is cheap differentiation and it is the right thing to do.
- **Copy uses the sport's vocabulary.** Gusset, flatlock, no ride-up during guard
  passes, IBJJF-appropriate. Not "buttery soft for your hot girl walk."
- **Squat-proof and grip-hold are the two functional claims** we make and must be able
  to back. Opacity-test the leggings. Wear-test the rashguard on the mat.

## Launch scope: 10 styles, 3 colorways, 6 sizes

Ten styles is deliberate. The failure mode you named — "a messy launch of random
products" — comes from launching 25 styles with no outfit logic, thin buy depth on all
of them, and a grid the shopper can't parse. Ten styles that snap together into
complete kits will outsell twenty-five that don't.

## Opening buy: how deep

Depth ratio across tiers — hero : core : depth = **2.5 : 1.5 : 1**.

Worked at three budget levels (units, all sizes and colorways combined per style):

| Style tier | Conservative | Standard | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each hero style (×3) | 250 | 500 | 900 |
| Each core style (×4) | 150 | 300 | 540 |
| Each depth style (×3) | 100 | 200 | 360 |
| **Total units** | **1,650** | **3,300** | **5,940** |

At a blended $46 AOV per unit and a 60% gross margin, the Standard buy represents roughly
$152k of retail value and roughly $61k of landed cost. Size that against your actual
capital before committing — this table is a shape, not a recommendation.

Rules regardless of level:
- **Never buy the limited colorway deep.** Bone at 50–60% the depth of Blackout.
- **Hold 15% of the buy back** as a week-three replenishment on whatever is selling.
- **Blackout is the safe colorway.** If cash is tight, cut Blood before you cut Blackout.

## SKU and barcode scheme

Lock this before you place the buy.

```
KKW-{CAT}-{STYLE}-{CW}-{SIZE}
```

- `KKW` — King Killers Women's
- `{CAT}` — `LEG` legging · `CMP` compression short · `SHT` fight short · `BRA` sports bra ·
  `RSH` rashguard · `TEE` tee · `TNK` tank · `SHD` sleeveless hoodie · `HOD` hoodie ·
  `SOK` socks · `KIT` bundle
- `{STYLE}` — style name, uppercase, no spaces
- `{CW}` — `BLK` · `BLD` · `BON`
- `{SIZE}` — `XS` `S` `M` `L` `XL` `2XL` (socks: `SM` `LXL`)

Example: `KKW-LEG-THRONE-BLK-M`

Barcodes: assign GTIN-13 at the variant level before the first PO. You will need them for
Google Shopping, Meta catalog, and any future wholesale or marketplace channel. Retrofitting
barcodes across 128 variants after launch is a bad afternoon.
